Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before we start working together.

What do you need from me to start?

Your target industry, location, ideal customer, company size, and which fields you need (company, contact role, website, business email).

How are emails verified?

I use email verification tools and public sources, then label each record with a status and source note so you know which ones are safe to use and which need review.

In what format do I receive the data?

Clean Excel or CSV files, or CRM-ready data. Fields are standardized and duplicates removed.

Do you send the outreach emails too?

Yes, I can support outreach and follow-up sequences, but only for relevant business contacts and in line with email regulations and platform rules.

Can you clean my existing list?

Yes. I remove duplicates, fix formatting, flag invalid or risky addresses and organize the list for your CRM.
